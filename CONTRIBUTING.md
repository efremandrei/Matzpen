# Correcting or submitting election positions

Use the [position evidence issue form](https://github.com/efremandrei/Matzpen/issues/new/choose) to propose a correction or a party-submitted answer. Include the exact question, the list, a dated public URL, and the passage that addresses the question. Party representatives should publish their answer on an official party site or verified official account and link to it; a GitHub comment alone does not establish a party position.

Editors compare the proposed source with the question as written and the existing evidence. They record whether the source supports, opposes, or conditionally supports the statement, check its date and identity, and keep conflicting evidence visible during review. A disputed position must not be scored until the conflict is resolved and documented. Every accepted change increments the feed revision, updates the changelog, receives a documented editorial review, and is signed before publication. Never place the private signing key in the repository.

The current bundled snapshot is adapted from the dated open dataset identified in [DATA_LICENSE.md](DATA_LICENSE.md). It includes public documentary evidence and direct statements. No party-submitted answer has been added to this first snapshot.
