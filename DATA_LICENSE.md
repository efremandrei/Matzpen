# Election data attribution

The election snapshot in `app/src/main/assets/content.json` and `docs/feed/content.json` adapts data from **מצפן הבחירה 2026** by Aidvocate, dataset version **2026-09-30**, source commit `4b304f9983f981992ecaf5a66cfc597155745d85`.

- Original work: https://bhirot26.online
- Open repository: https://github.com/dangelm/bhirot26-election-data
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Changes: selected the first 18 policy questions and omitted two coalition-strategy questions; removed positions supported solely by third-party reporting; omitted candidate rosters and quotes; added English and Arabic question translations to the snapshot, Russian question translations and list names in the app, and an app-specific scoring method. Source titles, dates, and links remain attached to scored positions. The original authors do not endorse this app.

Source documents and quotes linked from the dataset may have separate copyrights. Matzpen links to them rather than republishing quotes.
